# Data

The pipeline expects a single PDF, `allfile.pdf`, containing the concatenated official
Arabic regulations of the University of Basrah (examination and disciplinary rules,
student affairs, academic freedom and employee rights, women's affairs and equality,
environment and sustainability).

- **Colab:** upload it to `/content/allfile.pdf`.
- **Local:** place it at `data/allfile.pdf` and update the path in Section 6 of the notebook.

The regulations are official documents of the University of Basrah / the Iraqi Ministry of
Higher Education and Scientific Research. <Choose one:>
- *They are included here as `allfile.pdf` with permission.*  **or**
- *They are publicly available at <URL>; download them and merge into one PDF.*  **or**
- *They are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request.*

The evaluation question set used in the paper (`fixed_eval_set_n60_seed42.json`, n = 60,
seed = 42) can be placed in this folder to reproduce the exact reported queries.
